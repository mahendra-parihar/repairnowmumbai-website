Common LG Washing Machine Problems We Fix

  • OE error — water not draining, clogged drain pump or filter
  • IE error — drum not filling, inlet valve or water-tap issue
  • UE error — unbalanced load, heavy shaking during spin
  • dE / DE error — door not locking or not opening after the cycle
  • LE error — motor locked or inverter motor fault
  • Grinding or rumbling noise from worn drum bearings
  • Water leaking from the door gasket or base
  • Display or PCB not responding, machine not starting
  • Semi-automatic: spin dryer not spinning, wash timer or motor fault

How LG Machines Fail — and What We Check

Most LG front-load models sold in India use the Inverter Direct Drive motor, which connects straight to the drum with no belt. That removes belt problems, so when a direct-drive LG spins badly the fault is usually in the motor rotor and stator, the hall sensor, the inverter section of the PCB or the drum bearings.

LG top-loaders and semi-automatics fail differently: pulsator and clutch wear, drain-motor faults, worn spin-dryer belts and timer problems are far more common. Our technician first identifies which family your machine belongs to, then runs the LG diagnostic checks before quoting.

LG Washing Machine Repair — FAQs

What does the OE error mean on my LG washing machine?+
OE means the machine could not drain water within the expected time. The usual causes are a clogged drain filter, a blocked drain hose or a failing drain pump. Cleaning the filter at the bottom front often helps; if the error returns, the pump needs checking.
Why is my LG front-load washing machine shaking violently?+
Heavy vibration usually comes from an unbalanced load, an unlevelled machine, transit bolts left in place, or worn dampers and drum bearings. If the noise is a grinding sound, the bearings are the likely cause.
How much does LG washing machine repair cost in Mumbai?+
Basic repairs such as a drain pump, inlet valve or door lock usually cost ₹500–₹1,300 including the part. Inverter motor, PCB and bearing replacements are quoted after inspection, and you approve the price before we start.
Do you use genuine LG spare parts?+
We use genuine or OEM-quality spares, never refurbished parts. Every part and the labour carry our 90-day warranty.
Is my LG machine still under company warranty?+
If your machine is still within LG's warranty, use LG's authorised service first, since our repair may not be covered by the brand. RepairNow Mumbai is an independent repair service and is not affiliated with LG. For out-of-warranty machines, we usually reach you faster and quote fixed prices.
