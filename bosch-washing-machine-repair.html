Common Bosch Washing Machine Problems We Fix

  • F18 error — water not draining, pump or filter blocked
  • F17 error — water not filling within the set time
  • F21 error — motor not reaching speed, or motor fault
  • F16 error — door not closed or door-lock fault
  • F23 error — AquaStop leak protection triggered
  • F19 error — water not heating
  • E67 error — electronic module fault, machine not responding
  • Vibration or rumbling from worn bearings or dampers
  • Water on the floor from the door seal, pump or hose connections

What Sets Bosch Machines Apart When They Fail

Bosch front-loaders use the EcoSilence Drive brushless motor and a safety system called AquaStop, which shuts off the water supply when it detects a leak. That is why a leak often shows up as an F23 error instead of a flood, and why the fix may be in a hose, valve or pump seal rather than the drum.

Bosch machines also depend on a well-working electronic module. An F-code that keeps coming back after a reset can mean a sensor, wiring or module fault. We read the fault history, test the suspected part and only then quote, so you don't pay for parts that aren't the problem.

Bosch Washing Machine Repair — FAQs

What does F18 mean on a Bosch washing machine?+
F18 means the machine could not drain water within the expected time. The usual causes are a blocked pump filter, a kinked hose or a failing drain pump. Clean the filter behind the small flap at the bottom front; if F18 returns, the pump needs testing.
Why does my Bosch machine show F21?+
F21 points to the motor not reaching the required speed. This can be caused by worn carbon brushes, a motor fault, a heavy or unbalanced load, or the electronic module. A technician needs to test the motor and module before replacing anything.
What is the AquaStop system, and why is it giving an error?+
AquaStop is Bosch's leak protection. It stops water flow if it detects a leak, and the machine shows an error such as F23. We check hoses, the pump and the base for seepage, dry the sensor area and reset the system.
How much does Bosch washing machine repair cost in Mumbai?+
Common repairs such as a drain pump, inlet valve or door lock usually cost ₹500–₹1,500 with the part. Bosch spares tend to cost more than mass-market brands, and motor, bearing and module jobs are quoted after inspection.
Are you a Bosch authorised service centre?+
No. RepairNow Mumbai is an independent service and is not affiliated with Bosch. If your machine is within Bosch's warranty, use the brand's service first. For out-of-warranty machines we offer fixed-price repairs with a 90-day warranty.
