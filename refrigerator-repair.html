Common Reasons a Refrigerator Stops Cooling

A refrigerator may cool poorly because of blocked ventilation, dirty condenser coils, a damaged door gasket, a defrost-system fault or a problem with the thermostat, compressor or sealed refrigerant system. These symptoms can have different causes, so diagnosis should come before replacing parts or adding refrigerant. Keep the appliance ventilated and follow the manufacturer's care instructions.

Why has my refrigerator stopped cooling?+
Possible causes include dirty condenser coils, a door-seal problem, a defrost-system fault, a thermostat issue, or a compressor/refrigerant problem. A technician can inspect the appliance to identify the cause.

The page lists an approximate range of ₹1,800–₹3,200 for gas leak repair and refilling, depending on the refrigerator and refrigerant. The cause of the leak should be checked first; confirm the final quote before approving work.

RepairNow lists support for single-door, double-door, triple-door and side-by-side refrigerators, including frost-free and inverter-compressor models. Contact the team to confirm support for your model.
