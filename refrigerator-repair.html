Why Fridges Struggle in Mumbai Homes

High ambient humidity, frequent power fluctuations and dust accumulation on condenser coils are common reasons refrigerators lose cooling efficiency faster in Mumbai than in drier regions. We recommend a coil-cleaning service once every 6 months, especially for units placed in kitchens with limited ventilation.

Our Diagnostic Process

Our technician checks thermostat calibration, compressor current draw, refrigerant pressure and door seal integrity, then provides a fixed quote before starting repairs — so there are no surprises on the bill.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why has my refrigerator stopped cooling?+
The most frequent causes are a refrigerant gas leak, a faulty compressor, a defrost timer failure, or a blocked condenser coil covered in dust.
How much does refrigerator gas refilling cost?+
Gas refilling for a single or double-door refrigerator typically costs between ₹1,800–₹3,200 depending on compressor capacity and gas type, quoted after leak detection.
Do you repair side-by-side and double-door refrigerators?+
Yes, we service single-door, double-door, triple-door and side-by-side refrigerators, including frost-free and inverter compressor models.
Is it worth repairing an old refrigerator?+
Our technician will give you an honest repair-vs-replace recommendation based on the compressor's condition and the fridge's age before you commit to any cost.
