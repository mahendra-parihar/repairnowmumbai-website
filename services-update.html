Refrigerator Repair

Fridges in Mumbai lose cooling efficiency faster than average thanks to coastal humidity, dust on condenser coils and frequent power fluctuations. Our technicians diagnose the actual fault — not just the symptom — before quoting a fixed price.

  • Cooling problems — fridge not cooling, or freezer cold but fridge compartment warm
  • Compressor issues — running continuously without cooling, or not starting at all
  • Gas leakage — refrigerant leak detection and recharging (R134a, R600a)
  • Noisy refrigerator — buzzing, clicking or rattling from compressor or fan

Washing Machine Repair

Front-load, top-load and semi-automatic machines each fail differently — door seals and drain pumps on front-loaders, motors and timers on top-loaders. Our technicians carry spares for all three types.

  • Not spinning — worn belt, faulty motor or blown carbon brushes
  • Not draining — blocked or faulty drain pump after the wash cycle
  • Error codes — display panel fault codes on front-load models
  • Door problems — door not latching, or water leaking from the door seal

Dishwasher Repair

Mumbai's hard water speeds up limescale buildup on spray arms and heating elements, which is the most common cause of dishwashers losing cleaning performance over time.

  • Dishes not coming out clean — clogged spray arms or worn wash pump
  • Not draining — blocked drain hose or filter
  • Door not latching or closing properly
  • Error codes on digital display panel

Microwave Oven Repair

Microwaves store high-voltage charge even when unplugged — if yours is sparking or smells burnt, stop using it and call us rather than opening the casing yourself.

  • Not heating — faulty magnetron, capacitor or diode
  • Sparking or arcing sounds inside the cavity
  • Turntable not rotating
  • Display panel not lighting up or unresponsive buttons
