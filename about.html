About RepairNow Mumbai

Mumbai's home appliance repair partner since 2015 — built on verified technicians, fair pricing and same-day service.

Our Story

RepairNow Mumbai started in 2015 with a simple frustration: finding a trustworthy appliance repair technician in Mumbai often meant unreliable timing, unclear pricing and inconsistent quality. We set out to fix that — building a network of background-checked, trained technicians and a standardized pricing model so customers always know what they're paying for before a screwdriver is picked up.

Today, we've completed over 50,000 repairs across Mumbai's suburbs and South Mumbai, covering refrigerators, washing machines, dishwashers and microwave ovens.

Our Promise

  • Every technician is ID-verified and trained before visiting your home
  • You approve the repair cost before any work begins — no hidden charges
  • Only genuine spare parts are used, never duplicates
  • Every repair carries a 90-day warranty on parts and labour
  • Same-day service in most Mumbai locations

How We Select Our Technicians

Every technician goes through identity verification, a practical skills assessment, and a probation period under supervision before being allowed to service customers independently. We also run periodic customer satisfaction audits and retrain technicians who fall below our quality bar.
