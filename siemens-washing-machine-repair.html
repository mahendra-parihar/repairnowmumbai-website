Common Siemens Washing Machine Problems We Fix

  • Machine stopped mid-cycle with water still in the drum
  • F18 error — drain pump, filter or hose blockage
  • F17 error — slow or no water intake
  • F21 error — iQdrive motor speed fault
  • F23 error — leak protection triggered
  • E67 error — module fault, or display frozen
  • Door lock fault, door won't open after the cycle
  • Loud drum noise, bearing wear or damper failure
  • Wi-Fi / Home Connect not pairing on connected models

Servicing Premium Siemens Washers

Siemens washers are premium German-engineered machines built on the same technology platform as Bosch, so many fault codes overlap. Where they differ is in the top-end iQ500 and iQ700 models, which add extra sensors, load-sensing and connected features such as Home Connect.

Siemens machines use the iQdrive brushless motor, which is quiet and durable, but a fault usually means a motor-control issue that needs proper testing rather than guessing. Because spares for imported models are pricier, we diagnose first and show you what is failing before quoting, so you decide whether repair makes sense.

Siemens Washing Machine Repair — FAQs

My Siemens washing machine stopped mid-cycle. What should I check?+
First check that the tap is open, the drain hose isn't kinked and the door is fully shut. Then switch it off for a minute and restart. If the display shows F17, F18 or E67 again, a component needs testing.
What does the F18 error mean on a Siemens washer?+
F18 means the machine couldn't drain in time. A blocked pump filter is the commonest cause, followed by a kinked hose or a weak pump. Clean the filter first; if F18 comes back, book a check.
Do you repair imported Siemens washing machines?+
Yes, we work on both Indian-market and imported Siemens units. Spares for imported models can take a few days to arrive, and we'll tell you the likely wait when we quote.
Is repairing a Siemens machine worth it?+
Usually yes for drain, valve, door-lock and bearing jobs, since these machines are built to last well over ten years. Our technician gives an honest view if the repair cost approaches the price of a new machine.
Are you a Siemens authorised service centre?+
No. RepairNow Mumbai is an independent service and is not affiliated with Siemens. If your machine is within warranty, use the brand's service first. For out-of-warranty machines we offer fixed-price repair with a 90-day warranty.
