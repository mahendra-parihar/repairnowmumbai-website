How fast can a technician reach me in Mumbai?+
In most areas — Andheri, Bandra, Powai, Dadar, Thane and nearby — we dispatch a technician within 45–90 minutes of confirming your booking. For remote suburbs, it may take slightly longer.
Do you charge for the visit if I don't get the repair done?+
A nominal visiting/diagnostic charge applies, which is fully adjusted against the repair cost if you proceed with us.
Is there a warranty on repairs?+
Yes, every repair comes with a 90-day warranty covering both the replaced part and the labour involved.
Which brands and appliances do you service?+
We service all major brands — LG, Samsung, Whirlpool, IFB, Godrej, Bosch, Haier, Panasonic and more — across refrigerators, washing machines, dishwashers and microwave ovens.

We accept UPI, cards and cash. Payment is collected only after the repair is completed to your satisfaction.
