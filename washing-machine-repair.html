Common Washing Machine Issues We Fix

  • Machine not spinning or spinning very slowly
  • Water not draining after wash cycle
  • Excessive vibration or loud banging noise
  • Water leaking from the door or base
  • Machine not starting / no power
  • Error codes on front-load display panel
  • Drum not filling with water
  • Foul smell from drum (deep cleaning service)
  • Timer, PCB and control panel replacement (semi-automatic)

Front-Load vs Top-Load vs Semi-Automatic

Front-load machines commonly develop door-seal and drain-pump issues due to Mumbai's hard water and lint buildup, while top-load and semi-automatic machines more often face motor, belt and timer problems. Our technicians carry spares for all three types, so most repairs are completed in a single visit.

Our Repair Process

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my washing machine not spinning?+
A worn drive belt, faulty motor, blown carbon brushes, or an overloaded drum are the most common reasons a washing machine stops spinning.
How much does washing machine repair cost in Mumbai?+
Basic repairs (belt, drainage, control panel) typically range from ₹399–₹900. Motor or PCB replacement is quoted after inspection, usually ₹1,200–₹3,500.
Do you repair front-load and top-load machines?+
Yes, we repair fully-automatic front-load, top-load and semi-automatic washing machines across all major brands.
Can you fix a machine that's leaking water from the door?+
Yes — this is usually a worn door seal/gasket, which we can replace on the same visit in most cases.
