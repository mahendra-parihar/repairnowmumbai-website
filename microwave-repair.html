Common Microwave Problems We Fix

  • Microwave running but not heating food
  • Sparking or arcing sounds inside the cavity
  • Turntable not rotating
  • Display panel not lighting up or unresponsive buttons
  • Door not closing properly / door switch fault
  • Unusual burning smell during operation
  • Convection/grill mode not working
  • Loud humming or buzzing noise
  • Magnetron and high-voltage capacitor replacement

Important Safety Note

Microwave ovens store high-voltage charge even when unplugged. If your microwave is sparking, smells burnt, or has visible damage inside, stop using it and avoid opening the casing yourself — our trained technicians handle these repairs safely with proper discharge procedures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my microwave not heating food?+
A faulty magnetron, blown high-voltage capacitor, or a failed diode are the most common causes of a microwave that runs but doesn't heat.
Is it safe to repair a microwave that's sparking?+
Sparking is usually caused by a damaged mica plate or metal debris inside — stop using it immediately and have a technician inspect it, as microwaves carry high-voltage components that need professional handling.
Do you repair convection and grill microwaves too?+
Yes, we repair solo, grill and convection microwave ovens across all major brands.
How long does a typical microwave repair take?+
Most repairs — including magnetron or capacitor replacement — are completed within 30–45 minutes on-site.
