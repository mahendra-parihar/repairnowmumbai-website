Common Samsung Washing Machine Problems We Fix

  • 5E / 5C error — water not draining, blocked drain pump or hose
  • 4E / 4C error — water not filling, inlet valve or low tap pressure
  • UE / UB error — unbalanced load or machine shaking during spin
  • dE / dC error — door lock failure, door won't open after wash
  • 3E error — motor or tacho sensor fault
  • HE error — heater or temperature sensor fault
  • Loud noise from drum bearings or worn shock absorbers
  • Water leaking from the door seal, detergent drawer or hose
  • Top-load: wobble or pulsator issues, lid switch fault, machine not starting

Samsung Front-Load and Top-Load: Where the Faults Are

Samsung front-loaders use the Digital Inverter motor with a Diamond drum, and the faults we see most are clogged drain-pump filters, door-lock assemblies that stop latching, and drum-bearing wear on machines that have run for several years with unbalanced loads.

Samsung top-loaders run on a different design. Wobble-type wash action, suspension rods, lid switches and the clutch assembly are the usual trouble spots. We check the machine type first, because the same symptom, such as vibration, has a different cause on each.

Samsung Washing Machine Repair — FAQs

What does the 5E error mean on a Samsung washing machine?+
5E (or 5C on some models) means the machine can't drain. The usual causes are a clogged debris filter, a kinked or blocked drain hose or a weak drain pump. Cleaning the filter is the first step; if it still fails, the pump or its wiring needs testing.
Why won't my Samsung washing machine door open?+
The door stays locked if there is water still in the drum, a drain fault, or a worn door-lock assembly. Never force the door. Our technician drains the water safely and checks the lock and its sensor.
How much does Samsung washing machine repair cost in Mumbai?+
Common jobs such as a drain pump, inlet valve or door lock usually cost ₹500–₹1,300 with the part. Bearing, motor and PCB jobs are quoted after inspection. You approve the price before any work starts.
My Samsung machine shakes badly during spin. Is it serious?+
Not always. An unbalanced load or uneven floor is the easy cause. If it shakes even with a balanced load, worn suspension or shock absorbers, or a bearing about to fail, is likely, and it is better to fix early before the drum is damaged.
Is RepairNow Mumbai a Samsung authorised service centre?+
No. We are an independent repair service and are not affiliated with Samsung. If your machine is under Samsung's warranty, use the brand's service first. For out-of-warranty machines, we offer fast, fixed-price repairs with a 90-day warranty.
