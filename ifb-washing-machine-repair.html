Common IFB Washing Machine Problems We Fix

  • Water not draining, or a drain pump that hums but does not pump
  • Door stuck locked after the cycle ends

IFB Machines and Mumbai's Water

IFB is one of the most common front-load brands in Mumbai homes, with the Senator, Serena, Eva, Executive and Neo Diva series widely installed. Their most frequent complaints are drainage faults, door-lock failures and heating problems, and hard water makes the last two worse over the years.

Scale on the heating element causes the machine to trip or stop heating, and lint that collects around the drain pump causes the drain errors. On IFB top-loaders we usually see inlet valve, lid-lock and motor issues. We descale, clean and test the whole wash path instead of just swapping one part.

IFB Washing Machine Repair — FAQs

Why is my IFB washing machine not draining?+
The commonest causes are a clogged drain-pump filter, a blocked hose, or a failing drain pump. Front-loaders collect lint, coins and hair pins in the filter. We clean the filter, test the pump and replace it only if needed.
My IFB washing machine door is locked and won't open. What should I do?+
Don't force it. The door stays locked when water remains in the drum or when the lock assembly has failed. Switch the machine off, wait a few minutes, and if it stays locked call us. We drain it safely and test the lock.
Why does my IFB machine not heat water?+
Hard water scale on the heating element, a faulty thermostat or a burnt heater are the usual causes. Descaling fixes it when the element is healthy; otherwise it needs replacement.
How much does IFB washing machine repair cost in Mumbai?+
Most common repairs cost ₹450–₹1,400 with the part. Bearings, motors and PCBs are quoted after inspection, and you approve the price before we begin.
Are you an IFB authorised service centre?+
No. RepairNow Mumbai is an independent service and is not affiliated with IFB. If your machine is still under IFB's warranty, use the brand's service. Out-of-warranty machines are our speciality, with a 90-day warranty on parts and labour.
